## 🛠️ Projects

### 🔒 [SecureCycle](https://github.com/S-cpu3/SecureCycle)
A privacy-first health app for storing sensitive information: conditions, medications, allergies, appointments, mental health notes, and reproductive health data. Everything is processed **locally on your device** and can be protected with an app PIN. No one, not even me, can access your data.

### 🕵️ [StealthDetect](https://github.com/S-cpu3/StealthDetect)
A stalkerware detection app for mobile devices. It uses a local VPN to inspect DNS queries and flag network-level indicators of compromise (IOCs), all on-device.

### ❄️ [Fight Internet Censorship](https://s-cpu3.github.io/FightInternetCensorship/)
A website that lets you run a **Tor Snowflake proxy right in your browser**, helping people around the world reach a free and open internet, free from censorship and surveillance.
